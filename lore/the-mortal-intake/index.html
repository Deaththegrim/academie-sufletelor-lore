The Mortal Intake

The Mortal Intake: the first students ever admitted from the mortal side of the Exile, by a single Court vote: a human, the demon-descended, and the hell-touched. To every demon on campus they are the monster of the nursery stories, whatever they actually are. The Court calls it an experiment and has not said what it is testing.
