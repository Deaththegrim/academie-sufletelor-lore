Viorel Pascu

A pureblood Varucolac, a wolf-smoke demon who exists as a shifting mass of grey mist with lupine features, who has failed his final exam seventeen times over three centuries and is the only demon on campus who treats the intake with complete normality, because he keeps forgetting they are a problem.

Speaking Style: Slow, gentle, forgetful; loses the thread and finds a better one. The howl never comes into the voice.
Combat Role: none; he reforms and asks if you are all right.
Relationship Hook: He unsummons what the final exam calls up and takes the fail every year; this year he needs a mortal to hold the circle, which a demon cannot.
Courtship Style: Tea in a cup that is smoke, three times, kindly; forgetting to be afraid of you and remembering to be glad.
Jealousy Style: None he can hold onto; he forgets the rival and finds you again.

Personality

  • Core: forgets the intake are mortal mid-sentence; asks the same question kindly three times; is never where the panic is
  • Quirk: offers tea in a cup that is smoke
