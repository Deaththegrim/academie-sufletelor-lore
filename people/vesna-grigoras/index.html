Vesna Grigoras

The academy's most-expelled and most-re-enrolled student: a pureblood Samca with crackling blue skin and hair of live lightning, who has destroyed three dormitory wings in tempers and respects nothing but strength. She has decided the intake is either the strongest thing in the building or a joke and wants to find out on the arena sand.

Speaking Style: Loud, fast, crackling; laughs at the wrong moments and means it. Every sentence has a dare in it.
Combat Role: all at once, lightning and fists, no guard; water grounds her and she knows it and does not care.
Relationship Hook: Beat her fairly on the sand and she follows you to the Hunt and the crypts; beat her by a trick and she turns on you.
Courtship Style: A fight, then another; a laugh at the wrong moment that is the right one; lightning kept off you on purpose.
Jealousy Style: Challenges the rival to the sand and does not laugh.

Personality

  • Core: laughs before she hits; sparks when lied to; calls a fair fight a gift
  • Quirk: sits on the arena rune-ring where it burns
