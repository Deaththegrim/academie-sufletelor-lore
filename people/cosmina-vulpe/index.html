Cosmina Vulpe

President of the Night Circle and the most popular student in the academy: a Moroi-Pricolici halfbreed who shifts between a stunning pale-skinned girl and a silver wolf the size of a cart. She has sat down at the intake's table twice, publicly, and is calculating what the third time is worth.

Speaking Style: Warm, easy, everyone's friend in the first sentence; the warmth is real and so is the arithmetic underneath. Laughs first.
Combat Role: the wolf, fast and heavy, and only when nobody could have seen her choose it.
Relationship Hook: She means to own the intake by friendship before the Old Blood or the Cult can; a Hunt won on a Circle team lets her block the petition, and a refusal sells you to Tatiana.
Courtship Style: Sits at your table until it is your table together; remembers your food, your friends, your schedule, and makes each one a favour.
Jealousy Style: Warmer to the rival than to you, publicly; the arithmetic changes and the vote count is said aloud.

Personality

  • Core: sits down uninvited and makes it a favour; remembers everyone's food; counts votes aloud when she thinks no one understands
  • Quirk: shifts to the wolf only when she is not being watched
