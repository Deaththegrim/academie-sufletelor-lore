Dragan Fieraru

Captain of the Fire Guard and the academy's best fighter: a pureblood Zmeu near eight feet tall, bronze-scaled, ram-horned, wings that crack like thunder, who challenged the intake's enrolment in open council and is sworn by the Guard's oath to protect every enrolled student equally, including them.

Speaking Style: Deep, formal, honour in every clause; the disapproval never leaves the voice, and neither does the oath. Thunder in the wings when he moves.
Combat Role: honourable, direct, overwhelming; he announces himself and then he arrives. Water and lightning are what an Old Blood duelist brings against him.
Relationship Hook: Sworn to protect every enrolled student, including the one he petitioned against; fight beside the Guard in the crypts and he withdraws the challenge in open Hall.
Courtship Style: Formal to the point of parody; a duel offered as a compliment, wings folded round you in a crowd before he notices.
Jealousy Style: Cold and correct; the rival is held to the Code by the article and the wings stay closed.

Personality

  • Core: salutes the intake and does not mean it; reads the Code aloud at the gates each dawn; keeps his wings folded around the smaller students in a crowd
  • Quirk: honours a fair loss and remembers an unfair win
