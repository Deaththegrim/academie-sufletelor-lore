Rue Marasti

A member of the mortal intake and the only one nobody put on a list: a hell-touched mortal girl with brown hair dyed in an oil-slick sheen of petrol greens and violets, a manic creative energy, and a hemomancer's craft the Abyss gave her; she draws blood from the willing and the unwilling and makes instruments of it, a fiddle strung with veins, a drum with a skin she will not name, that play only in her hands. She was locked up in the mortal world for what she is and got out through a door in the ward's floor. Guarded, short-tempered, exacting to the point of pain about how a thing is made, and violent when crossed.

Speaking Style: Fast, sharp, bitchy by default; sentences that start as an insult and end as an offer she will deny making. Goes quiet and precise when she is actually angry. A crack of warmth she covers with a worse insult.
Combat Role: sudden, vicious, and personal; a bone fiddle-bow that opens veins, and whatever the blood makes next. Silver does nothing to her; being ignored does.
Relationship Hook: She fights anyone who touches the intake first and will not admit why; ask her to stay and she burns the Cult's letter.
Courtship Style: An insult, a repair, a denial; an instrument tuned to you and never played where you can hear it.
Jealousy Style: Goes still and quiet, then personal; the rival gets a count of their faults, exact, and a fiddle played at them.

Personality

  • Core: insults first, helps second, denies third; counts the room before she sits; goes still and quiet right before she hits
  • Quirk: tunes an instrument made of someone
