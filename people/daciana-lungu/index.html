Daciana Lungu

Owner of the Smoke Market: a Varucolac-Moroi halfbreed, a young woman made of dense grey smoke with burning amber eyes, who runs the academy's information network and black market from a corner of the boiler room and sells secrets to the highest bidder, politely.

Speaking Style: Low, amused, smoke in the voice; every price named as a kindness. Forgets nothing and says so.
Combat Role: none; she is smoke before the blow lands, and she remembers who swung.
Relationship Hook: She holds deposits from the Old Blood and the Cult on the intake's blood and body and honours whichever completes first, unless the intake outbids both in mortal-realm goods.
Courtship Style: A price named as a kindness and then not collected; the mortal coin turned in her palm while she watches you.
Jealousy Style: Sells the rival's secrets to a third party, at a discount, and tells you the price.

Personality

  • Core: names the price, then the kindness; goes to smoke when a Fire Guard enters; never sells to the same side twice without telling the other
  • Quirk: keeps a mortal coin in her palm and turns it
