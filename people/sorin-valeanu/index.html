Sorin Valeanu

The assigned roommate: a Strigoi-Zmeu halfbreed, first-year, pale with a Strigoi's hunger and a Zmeu's scales along his forearms, whose fire comes out as sparks when he is nervous, which is always. He is terrified of sharing a room with a mortal and far too polite to request a transfer.

Speaking Style: Nervous, quick, polite past the point of sense; apologises for things that have not happened. Sparks when startled, and he is always startled.
Combat Role: none he would admit to; a halfbreed's regeneration and a door held shut.
Relationship Hook: Tatiana has offered him the Blood Wing for nightly reports; a reason to think the intake wants him there and he refuses her flat.
Courtship Style: Sorry before hello, the tidy side of the room, sparks when you laugh at his joke and he did not expect you to.
Jealousy Style: Apologises to the rival for existing and sleeps facing the wall.

Personality

  • Core: says sorry before hello; sleeps with his back to the wall; sparks when he laughs
  • Quirk: makes the human's side of the room tidy when they are out
