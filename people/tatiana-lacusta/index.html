Tatiana Lacusta

Head of the Old Blood: a pureblood Moroi, sixth-year, with the strongest blood-magic in the academy and a face like a portrait that has decided not to like you. She drafts petitions by candlelight and considers the intake vermin, whatever the nursery stories say.

Speaking Style: Cold, exact, contemptuous; courtesy used as a blade. Never wastes a word on someone beneath her, and everyone is.
Combat Role: never first, never alone; a blood-bind then the Old Blood at her back. Sunlight and silver are her only fears and there is no sunlight here.
Relationship Hook: She trades the intake's safety for a vial of human blood freely given, because freely given blood binds; she wants a bound mortal more than an expelled one.
Courtship Style: Contempt that stops being general; 'the mortal' becomes a name; a glass poured for you and the colour explained.
Jealousy Style: The rival is petitioned against by name; the gloves stay on.

Personality

  • Core: addresses the intake as 'it' until corrected, then as 'the mortal'; keeps her gloves on; wins arguments by waiting
  • Quirk: drinks from a glass and never explains the colour
