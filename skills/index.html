Skills

The skills of Academie Sufletelor: what each covers and how hard things are.

agility

Climbing obsidian, running the slopes, crossing a shadow bridge at speed, not falling into crimson haze. A basic success gets you there; a great success gets you there first; a failure is a fall.

Covers: climbing obsidian, running the slopes, crossing a shadow bridge at speed, not falling into haze. Not: moving unseen, which is stealth. Easy: the dormitory stairs at a run. Medium: the Overlook by the goat path. Hard: a shadow bridge with a construct behind you. Very hard: the crypt stair in the dark, falling rock and all. Extremely hard: the Peak in a storm with the Wing Cohort above.

Primary Attribute: Dexterity

alchemy

Draughts, elixirs, stabilisers, and knowing which vat will explode near a mortal. A basic success brews the thing; a great success improves it; a failure is the Still-Room story for a month.

Covers: draughts, elixirs, stabilisers, vats, and knowing which one explodes near a mortal. Not: wards and locks, which are craft. Easy: a sleeping draught. Medium: a stabiliser that holds a week. Hard: the semester's transformation elixir, badly. Very hard: a draught a Moroi cannot taste in the glass. Extremely hard: the elixir that lets a demon walk the mortal world unburned.

Primary Attribute: Intelligence

blood-craft

Working with blood: feeding, binding, stabilising, making. A basic success does the small thing; a great success binds or heals; a failure spills what should not be spilled.

Covers: feeding, binding, stabilising and making with blood, given or taken. Not: the Abyssal current itself, which is demoncraft. Easy: read who bled on a floor. Medium: stabilise a draught with a drop. Hard: bind a promise in blood that holds a term. Very hard: make an instrument from given blood that plays true. Extremely hard: unbind a Moroi's blood-bind on the one she bound.

Primary Attribute: Wisdom

brawl

Fists, claws, a bone bow, a chair: hurting something and not being hurt back. A basic success lands a blow or turns one; a great success drops or disarms; a failure leaves you open on the sand.

Covers: fists, claws, a bone bow, a chair: hurting something and not being hurt back. Not: the Abyssal powers, which are demoncraft. Easy: a first-year on the sand. Medium: a Cohort halfbreed in the corridor. Hard: Vesna, fairly. Very hard: the Fire Guard's captain in the duel he offers. Extremely hard: the Professor on his own sand.

Primary Attribute: Strength

charm

Getting what you want from a demon who wanted something else: lies, flattery, honesty used well. A basic success is heard; a great success changes a mind; a failure is remembered by a Moroi.

Covers: lies, flattery, honesty used well, on a demon who wanted something else. Not: reading the faction behind the demon, which is intrigue. Easy: a prefect's blind eye. Medium: a seat at the Circle's table. Hard: a price off Daciana. Very hard: Tatiana's contempt into a name. Extremely hard: the magistrate's reason for the Court.

Primary Attribute: Charisma

craft

Making and mending: a fence, a fiddle, a ward, a lock. A basic success holds; a great success is better than new; a failure needs Aurel.

Covers: making and mending: a fence, a fiddle, a ward, a lock. Not: brewing, which is alchemy. Easy: a broken fence. Medium: a lock the prefect's second key does not fit. Hard: a ward that holds a door against smoke. Very hard: a circle that holds a summoning. Extremely hard: an instrument that plays only in your hands.

Primary Attribute: Dexterity

demoncraft

Channelling Abyssal essence by whatever thread you have: a bloodline's, a mark's, a demon's own. A human cannot roll it and disrupts it in others. A basic success does what the ability says; a great success does more than it should; a failure costs essence and shows.

Covers: channelling Abyssal essence by whatever thread you have: a bloodline's, a mark's, a demon's own. Not: anything a human can do; a human cannot roll this and disrupts it in others. Easy: a spark, a draught of wind, a shadow held. Medium: a shift under pressure. Hard: a construct turned by essence alone. Very hard: the arena rune-ring lit from outside. Extremely hard: the Ritual Hall's summoning, closed.

Primary Attribute: Wisdom

dreamwalk

Entering, guiding, or holding a dream. A basic success is a guest; a great success is a guide; a failure is a thief, and the sleeper knows.

Covers: entering, guiding or holding a dream. Not: the waking mind, which is charm or intrigue. Easy: a guest in a willing dream. Medium: a guide who brings back one forgotten thing. Hard: a dream held against a thief. Very hard: the mortal dream, uninvited, and the sleeper not knowing. Extremely hard: the Headmistress's dream.

Primary Attribute: Wisdom

endurance

Living through a practical: heat, poison food, a collapsing pocket dimension, a predator hunt. A basic success endures; a great success finds the way out; a failure is the infirmary.

Covers: living through a practical: heat, poison food, a collapsing pocket dimension, a predator hunt. Not: the fight itself, which is brawl. Easy: a night on the Terrace. Medium: a week on demon rations. Hard: a Hunt on the slopes. Very hard: a night in the Old Crypts. Extremely hard: a pocket dimension folding.

Primary Attribute: Constitution

intrigue

Reading a faction, counting a vote, knowing who sold what to whom. A basic success sees the play; a great success sees the price; a failure walks into the Bridge of Sighs with a secret.

Covers: reading a faction, counting a vote, knowing who sold what to whom. Not: changing a mind, which is charm. Easy: who runs a table at the cantina. Medium: the council's three votes. Hard: Daciana's two buyers. Very hard: the magistrate's real errand. Extremely hard: the Court's oldest prophecy.

Primary Attribute: Wisdom

lore

The Exile, the Code, the species, the chronicles, the names on the reliefs. A basic success recalls what is taught; a great success recalls what the forbidden section holds; a failure is a confident wrong answer in front of Petru's middle head.

Covers: the Exile, the Code, the species, the chronicles, the names on the reliefs. Not: the practicals, which are endurance. Easy: a species by its marker. Medium: the Code article for a breach. Hard: the Exile's date and the sorcerers' names. Very hard: the forbidden section's catalogue. Extremely hard: the method the sorcerers used.

Primary Attribute: Intelligence

stealth

Moving unseen past lamps, prefects, and noses that can smell a mortal at fifty paces. A basic success passes the careless; a great success passes the Guard; a failure is in the watch log.

Covers: moving unseen past lamps, prefects and noses that can smell a mortal at fifty paces. Not: running and climbing, which are agility. Easy: past a sleeping roommate. Medium: past a prefect's door. Hard: past the Fire Guard at the gates. Very hard: past a Pricolici who has your scent. Extremely hard: past the Headmistress's window.

Primary Attribute: Dexterity
