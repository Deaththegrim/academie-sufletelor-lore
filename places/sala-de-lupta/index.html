Sala de Lupta

[Recommended Level: 3-20 · Danger: Severe]

The Hall of Battle: a sand arena under a dome of warding runes that stop injuries from being lethal, and side rooms that can simulate any ground, including a mortal-world village. Professor Mircea runs his classes here like a siege. Lethal combat is permitted nowhere else on campus.

Areas

  • The Arena Floor: Black sand inside a ring of runes that glow when a blow lands. The floor remembers every fight fought on it and sometimes replays one.
  • The Professor's Rail: The iron rail above the floor where Mircea watches with a wolf's head on a man's shoulders and a slate of names.
  • The Simulation Rooms: Side chambers that become forest, cliff, crypt or, at Mircea's order, a mortal village with wooden houses and a church, which unsettles every demon who trains in it.

People here

  • Professor Mircea Obancea (Pricolici)
  • Vesna Grigoras (Samca)
