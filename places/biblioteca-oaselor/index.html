Biblioteca Oaselor

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 2-16 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The Library of Bones, built inside the fossilised ribcage of a dead leviathan: reading floors along the ribs, texts in every demonic tongue, and a forbidden section behind a gate of vertebrae. Petru Albescu works the desk and argues with himself about the filing.

Areas

  • The Desk: The librarian's desk at the spine: three ledgers, one for each of Petru's heads, and a bell that summons all three.
  • The Forbidden Gate: A gate of fused vertebrae before the Sectiunea Interzisa, warded to burn any student who touches it and, it turns out, not warded against humans at all.
  • The Reading Ribs: Tiers of desks along the leviathan's ribs, lamps of cold flame, students of every species pretending not to watch the intake read.
